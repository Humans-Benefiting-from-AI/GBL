About GBL

Girls Basketball League — a place where athletic excellence and personal growth share the same court.

Our story

Basketball that builds more than skill

GBL was created for girls who want serious basketball in a community-minded environment. Like the best youth programs, we believe the game teaches life: how to compete with dignity, how to lift a teammate, and how to keep showing up when the drill gets hard.

Led by coach Malkie Schulman, GBL runs league and clinic programming in Jerusalem with a clear standard — effort first, improvement always, and a culture where every girl belongs.

Our mission

Develop confident female athletes through high-quality coaching, meaningful competition, and values that travel far beyond the gym.

  • ✓Train the whole player — body, mind, and middot
  • ✓Create safe, ambitious spaces for girls to compete
  • ✓Build lasting friendships through shared challenge

Leadership

Meet Coach Malkie

MS

Malkie Schulman

Head Coach & Organizer · GBL

Malkie organizes and coaches the Girls Basketball League and Clinic at Nachal Refaim 17. Parents and players know her as the steady presence behind registration, team placement, and the day-to-day culture of the program — demanding excellence while making sure girls feel supported and seen.

The GBL way

What we emphasize every session

Compete with heart

Play hard, play fair, and stay locked in from warm-up to final whistle.

Grow together

Celebrate assists as much as buckets. Great teammates make great seasons.

Own the process

Show up on time, listen well, and treat every drill as a chance to level up.

Programs

Two paths. One standard.

Choose the League for competitive team play (grades 9–11) or the Clinic for skills-first development (grades 8–9). Both are coached by Malkie and rooted in the same GBL culture.
