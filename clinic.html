GBL Clinic

Skills-focused training for grades 8–9 — perfect for building foundations, confidence, and a love for the game.

Tuesday nights

Learn the game the right way

The clinic is designed for girls who want structured skill work in a supportive setting. Whether she is new to basketball or returning for another season, Malkie coaches fundamentals, footwork, shooting form, and team habits with clarity and energy.

Sessions run Tuesday nights from 8:00–8:45 PM at Nachal Refaim 17, starting October 6th, 2026 (after Sukkot).
