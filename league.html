GBL League

Competitive weekly basketball for grades 9–11 — tryouts, team placement, and game-ready coaching with Malkie Schulman.

2026 / 2027 season

Show up. Compete. Belong to a team.

The league is built for girls who want real basketball: structured sessions, leveled teams, and a coach who holds a high standard while keeping the game fun. Week one includes tryouts and warmups so every player lands on the right roster.

Sessions run one hour per week — either 7:00–8:00 PM or 8:00–9:00 PM depending on team placement. The season kicks off Wednesday, October 7th (after Sukkot).

How it works

From registration to first tip-off

1

Register online

Share player details, school, grade, experience, and jersey preferences. Parents complete the waiver at signup.

2

Week-one tryouts

Warmups and evaluation help place each girl on a team that matches her level and keeps play competitive.

3

Train & compete

Weekly sessions focus on skills, spacing, decision-making, and team habits — with a culture of effort first.
